Classic Mint Tea

More than just a drink, Moroccan mint tea is a symbol of hospitality and friendship. This sweet, refreshing green tea infused with fresh spearmint is the heart and soul of Moroccan culture.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons Chinese gunpowder green tea
  • 5 cups boiling water
  • 1 large bunch fresh spearmint (about 2 cups), plus extra sprigs for garnish
  • 1/2 cup sugar (or to taste)

Instructions

  1. Rinse & Prep: Rinse the gunpowder green tea leaves with a little boiling water, then discard the water.
  2. Brew: Add sugar and the remaining boiling water to the teapot with the rinsed leaves.
  3. Infuse: Add the large bunch of fresh spearmint and let it steep for 3-5 minutes over low heat.
  4. Aerate & Serve: Pour the tea into glasses from a high height to create foam, then garnish with a mint sprig.
